# Code of conduct

Forja is made by people with different backgrounds, skills and first languages. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas and code, never about people. Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and take feedback the same way.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or stalking, in public or in private.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, body, ability, background or beliefs.
- Sexual language or imagery directed at people, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or events.

## Where it applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and chats, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, report it privately to the maintainers. Until the project has a dedicated address, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([@PauloRTesJr](https://github.com/PauloRTesJr)). Say what happened, where and when, with links where you have them. Reports are kept confidential, and the person reporting is not identified to the person reported without their consent.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and act in proportion to it: a private note, a public warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent one. A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in deciding it.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
